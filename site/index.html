How I help

Clear information. A credible story. A prepared team.

The work starts with what already exists — the strengths of the business and the information already on file. From there I align the commercial story with the financial evidence, and set out plainly what still needs explaining, gathering or reconciling before someone outside the business asks.

Beyond the numbers

Show what is working. Explain what needs context.

Good preparation captures both sides honestly: the commercial strengths that deserve to stand out, and the matters a funder or buyer will reasonably want to understand.

Earnings quality

What does underlying, sustainable profit actually look like once one-off items and adjustments are understood?

Revenue and customers

How repeatable is the revenue, how concentrated is it, and what does that mean for the story being told?

Margins and operations

What drives margin at the level it sits, and how consistent is that against how the business is run day to day?

Working capital and cash

How does profit convert to cash, and what does a normal level of working capital look like for this business?

Balance sheet and commitments

What debt, debt-like items and commitments exist, and are they clearly understood and evidenced?

Forecast and opportunity

Are the assumptions behind the forecast reasonable, consistent, and supportable with evidence?

Strengths to demonstrate

The parts of the story that are already strong and simply need to be brought into focus, clearly and with evidence.

Matters requiring explanation

Areas where the numbers or the story need context so they read clearly rather than raise questions on their own.

Information still to assemble

Reconciliations, schedules or records that don't yet exist in a form ready to show someone outside the business.
